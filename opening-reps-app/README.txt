OPENING REPS - install it as an app on your iPhone

What's in this folder
  index.html              the whole trainer (board, 108 lines, sounds, voice coach)
  manifest.webmanifest    app name, icon and full-screen settings
  sw.js                   makes it work offline after the first visit
  *.png                   home-screen icons (4 files)

Step 1 - put it online (free, about 5 minutes, do this on your MacBook)
  1. Go to github.com and sign in (create a free account if needed).
  2. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name it: opening-reps
     Set it to Public and click "Create repository".
  3. On the new page, click "uploading an existing file", then "choose your files".
  4. Select ALL 8 files from the unzipped folder (Cmd+A on Mac, or "Select" then
     tap each file on iPhone) and click "Commit changes". There are no folders.
  5. Open Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)", then Save.
  6. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is now at:
     https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/opening-reps/

Step 2 - install it on your iPhone
  1. Open that link in SAFARI (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
  3. Open it from the new icon: it runs full screen, like a normal app.
  4. Open it once with internet; after that it also works offline.

On your MacBook (optional)
  Safari: File > Add to Dock.   Chrome: the install icon in the address bar.

Make the voice coach sound human
  iPhone: Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > English >
  download a voice marked "Enhanced" or "Premium" (e.g. Siri voices, Serena, Daniel).
  Mac: System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > System voice > Manage Voices.
  Then in the app, tap "Voice settings" and pick it.

Notes
  - Progress is saved on each device separately.
  - To update the app later, upload the new index.html to the same repository.
    The app picks up the new version the next time it opens with internet.
  - Make sure the phone's silent switch is off to hear move sounds.
